# Module card - WaveBank (waveform cluster, including LeCaine)

Bank wire value **5**: bank A, bank B, or bank C as `WaveBank (alias of B)` `[G]`
`docs/HakenMidi.h:1569`. Guide sections 6.8 and 6.8.1, "Multipurpose Banks: WaveBank (Waveform
Cluster)" and "LeCaine WaveBank" `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3230`, `:3270`. Card
schema, shared facts, the module-name spelling and what the alias form means: `index.md`,
which this card references and never restates.

> Citations of the form `analysis/findings/...` are the 2026-10-26 research pass's
> working files and are **not shipped**; the data behind a `[C]` claim is
> `factory_catalog.json`. See `index.md`, "Research-pass citations are not shipped".

## TL;DR

A cluster of up to five bandlimited wave generators with independent frequency columns - the
EaganMatrix's supersaw `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3235-3243`. The columns are fixed
(`index.md`, shared fact 2).

The waveform itself is **not a matrix column**: it is the bank-param id (`index.md`, shared
fact 4), and selecting LeCaine replaces the whole column map with six harmonic amplitudes plus
duty plus fundamental `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3288-3305`.

Leaving `F2`..`F5` blank is the documented way to use fewer than five waves `[G]`
`docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3241-3243`, so blank columns here are a control, not a trap.

`[C]` 97 of the 668 factory presets run a WaveBank, plus 1 more whose bank C mirrors bank B's
(`census.json`, `modules_used_including_defaults`).

## Key Decisions

- LeCaine is carded as a **second column map under one bank value**, because that is exactly
   what it is: one bank-param value, a completely different matrix layout `[G]`
   `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3288-3305`.
- The LeCaine column positions are taken from the corpus rather than the guide's table, which
   prints two different columns with the same label - see section 1b `[C]`
   (`analysis/findings/corpus-module-wiring.md` S8).

## Open Questions / Risks

- `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3245-3247` defines the duty cycle only in terms of
   square ("if the WaveBank has Square selected"); what it does to a sawtooth or a triangle is
   not stated. That is the merged device plan's DT13.
- `[I]` The guide describes nine labelled parameters for eight columns, with the weighting and
   the duty-offset parameters both unnumbered; the header resolves it by making them one
   column-mode toggle apart (`index.md`, shared fact 3) - the inference is that they share
   column 8, which the guide never states.

## 1. What every column means

WaveBank has **two** column maps under one bank value, chosen by the bank-param id
(`index.md`, shared fact 4) `[G]` `docs/HakenMidi.h:1591-1597`.

### 1a. Normal WaveBank (sawtooth, square, triangle)

| Col (bank A) | Label | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (27) | `A` | "Amplitude of the WaveBank waveform cluster. If the amplitude is 0, no waves are generated (conserves DSP processing)." | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3235-3237` |
| 2 (28) | `F1` | "Frequency (in kHz) for the first wave generator in the wave cluster" | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3239` |
| 3..6 (29-32) | `F2`..`F5` | Frequency of the second through fifth generator: "If a column is left blank, fewer waves will be generated by the WaveBank" | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3241-3243` |
| 7 (33) | `D` | Duty cycle, 0 to 1 or equivalently 0 to -1: "Value 0 is normal; if the WaveBank has Square selected, 0 is a 50% duty cycle square, and nonzero is a pulse wave" | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3245-3247` |
| 8 (34) | `W` or `oD` | `W` = weighting of the wave amplitudes: 0 = equal, up to 1 favours even-numbered waves, down to -1 favours odd-numbered. `oD` = duty offset, "added once to the second wave in the WaveBank, twice to the third, etc." Which one the column carries is a column-mode toggle (`index.md`, shared fact 3) | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3249-3254` |

A second WaveBank is available in bank B or bank C `[G]`
`docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3256`.

### 1b. LeCaine WaveBank

Selecting LeCaine replaces the map with an additive square-wave organ whose partials are at
the Le Caine multiples 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 8 `[G]`
`docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3272-3273`, `:3288-3298`.

| Col (bank A) | Label | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1..6 (27-32) | `A1 A2 A3 A4 A5 A8` | Amplitude of the square wave at 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x and 8x the fundamental | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3288-3298` |
| 7 (33) | duty | "Duty cycle for LeCaine square waves generated by the WaveBank. Values range from 0 to 1 (or equivalently, 0 to -1). Value 0 is normal" | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3300-3302`, position `[C]` (`analysis/findings/corpus-module-wiring.md` S8) |
| 8 (34) | `F` | "The Fundamental frequency of the LeCaine square wave" | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3305`, position `[C]` (`analysis/findings/corpus-module-wiring.md` S8) |

`[C]` The guide's LeCaine table prints the duty column's label as `A4`, colliding with the 4x
amplitude column, and gives no column numbers at all. The corpus settles both positions:
`Basic LeCaine` puts the predefined pitch code on **column 8** and the macro-controlled duty on
**column 7**, with columns 2..6 carrying five amplitude offsets
(`analysis/findings/corpus-module-wiring.md` S8). The guide's own prose description of that
preset matches cell for cell `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3313-3315`, verbatim: "Uses a
LeCaine WaveBank with ratioed offsets on the Square Wave amplitudes controlled by a Macro
Controller plus a second LeCaine Wavebank using fixed amplitude offsets".

Background on why the waveform is square only `[G]`
`docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3274-3275`, verbatim: "The Le Caine was based on a high
frequency tube circuit, which then divided down frequencies via flip-flop (which is why the
wave is square only)." The duty cycle "was not part of his original design" `[G]`
`docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3273`.

## 2. Units, useful ranges, blank-column behaviour

| Column | Units | What blank (= 0) does | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A`, or `A1`..`A8` on LeCaine | linear amplitude | **silent and free**: no waves are generated | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3235-3237` |
| `F1` | **kHz** | no first wave | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3239` |
| `F2`..`F5` | kHz | **a legitimate control**: that wave is simply not generated, so four saws means `F1`..`F4` written and `F5` blank | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3241-3243` |
| `D` | 0..1, equivalently 0..-1 | 0 is explicitly "normal", a 50% duty square; blank is correct | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3245-3247` |
| `W` | -1..+1 | 0 = equal weighting across the waves; neutral | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3249-3251` |
| `oD` | offset on `D` | no per-wave duty offset; neutral | `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3253-3254` |
| LeCaine `F` | kHz | silence `[I]` - the inference being that a zero fundamental leaves the whole divider chain at zero |

**Blank-column verdict.** WaveBank is the friendliest bank for blanks: `A`, `D`, `W`, `oD` and
`F2`..`F5` are each either neutral or a documented "fewer waves" control at zero, and there is
no blank-is-destructive column in the normal map `[G]`
`docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3235-3254`.

`[C]` Measured column occupancy over 98 WaveBank bank slots: c1 98%, c2 98%, c3 95%, c4 76%,
c5 57%, c6 51%, c7 62%, c8 46% - the documented "leave a column blank for fewer waves"
behaviour, visible as a decay across the frequency columns
(`analysis/findings/corpus-module-wiring.md` S7).

## 4. DSP cost class

**Light to medium, gated by `A` and by how many frequency columns are filled.** The guide
gives no figure but states both levers: at `A` = 0 nothing is generated `[G]`
`docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3235-3237`, and a blank frequency column generates one fewer
wave `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3241-3243`.

A quality note that doubles as a cost comparison: the square WaveBank is "strictly alias-free"
where the PWM oscillator "can alias if you aren't careful" and "uses about twice the
processing" of a bit-crushed sine `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:2149`, `:2151-2152`.
`[I]` So for clean detuned squares a WaveBank is both cleaner and cheaper than five PWM
oscillator slots - the inference is a direct reading of those two sentences together.

## 5. Typical wiring

Cell orientation is `column <- row = value`, the orientation `set_matrix_point(row, col,
value)` takes `[G]` `eagan_mcp/server.py:255`.

`Boson Particles Wbl` - a harmonic stack from one pitch letter and four integer constants
`[C]` (`factory_catalog.json`, `wiring`):

```
bank: A = WaveBank param=triangle ; B = SineBank ; C = SummedDelay
Bank A 1 (A)  <- Direct + = z^2
Bank A 2 (F1) <- Direct + = X
Bank A 3 (F2) <- Direct + = X, Direct x = 2
Bank A 4 (F3) <- Direct + = X, Direct x = 3
Bank A 5 (F4) <- Direct + = X, Direct x = 4
Bank A 6 (F5) <- Direct + = X, Direct x = 5
Bank A 7 (D)  <- Direct + = z^2, Direct x = 0.9
Bank A 8 (W)  <- Direct + = B          B = W SG1 1.0 [Sine]
```

`[C]` Harmonics 1 to 5 come from one predefined pitch code plus four integer constants on
`Direct x`; amplitude and duty cycle both come from the predefined pressure-squared code. All
98 WaveBank slots in the corpus drive the amplitude column.

`Basic LeCaine` - two LeCaine banks with ratioed amplitudes `[C]`
(`factory_catalog.json`, `wiring`):

```
bank: A = WaveBank cm1=5 cm2=0 ; B = WaveBank cm1=4 cm2=2 ; recirc = Modulated Delay
Bank A 1 <- Direct + = 1              Bank B 1 <- Direct + = 1
Bank A 2..6 <- Direct + = 1, Direct - = D / E / F / G / H
Bank B 2..6 <- Direct + = 0.5 / 0.25 / 0.125 / 0.075 / 0.03
Bank A 7 <- Direct + = K       <-- duty
Bank A 8 <- Direct + = X       <-- fundamental frequency
Bank B 8 <- Direct + = A       <-- fundamental plus a macro detune
  D/E/F/G/H = W*(X+Y+Z), W = Constant 1.0 x macro iii ; Y = 0.6/0.5/0.4/0.3/0.2
  K = W Constant 0.5 x macro iii     (duty, on the same macro as the amplitude offsets)
```

`Choir and Ext` uses a WaveBank as a four-voice chorus source, with `F2`..`F5` driven by four
phase-offset shape-generator taps on one macro `[C]` (`factory_catalog.json`, `wiring`).
`Organzilla`, `Squaresville`, `Bass Monster` and `Cowell Triangles` are the other WaveBank
presets with a shipped wiring block; `Bass Monster` at 12 cells and `Cowell Triangles` at 11
are the densest `[C]` (`factory_catalog.json`, `wiring`).

Device-verified chain already on record: bank A WaveBank with four detuned saws, frequencies
at the pitch code plus small offsets and amplitude on pressure, feeding a BiqMouth at .25, plus
a fifth generator at half pitch for a sub-octave on column 32 `[D]` `CLAUDE.md:128-130` and
`RECIPES.md:166-167`, 2026-10-25, fw 10.81. `[I]` Note what that construction shows: the
sub-octave is the fifth generator tuned an octave down, not a separate module.

## 6. Known traps

1. **The waveform is not in the matrix**, and the default is square rather than sawtooth - see
   the conflict in section 3 `[G]` `docs/HakenMidi.h:1594-1598`.
2. **Selecting LeCaine rewrites the column map.** The same eight cells mean six harmonic
   amplitudes plus duty plus fundamental instead of amplitude plus five frequencies plus duty
   plus weighting, so a matrix dump cannot be read without knowing the bank-param value `[G]`
   `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3288-3305`.
3. **`D` on a sawtooth or a triangle is undefined by the guide**, which describes duty only for
   square `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3245-3247`.
4. **`W` and `oD` share column 8.** Writing "weighting" into a bank whose column-mode toggle
   selects the duty offset writes a duty offset instead `[G]`
   `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3249-3254`.
5. **WaveBank uses the shared delay memory**, so a WaveBank plus a long bank C delay compete
   for one resource `[G]` `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:4157`; `index.md` shared fact 7 owns
   the budget and its consequence.
6. **Five waves is the maximum**, so "supersaw" here means five generators `[G]`
   `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3241-3243`.
7. **LeCaine partials are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 8 - there is no 6x or 7x**, so a request for all
   harmonics up to 8 cannot be met with LeCaine `[G]`
   `docs/EaganMatrixUserGuide.txt:3272-3273`.
